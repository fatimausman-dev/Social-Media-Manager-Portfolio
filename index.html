Reel script · 15 seconds

The 2-minute kitchen reset

Hook (0–2s)Your kitchen is not messy. It just has no reset routine.

Body (2–12s)Clear the counter. Spray, wipe, dry. Empty the sink. Bin the rubbish. Four moves, two minutes.

Call to action (12–15s)Send this to someone who cooks and never cleans.
